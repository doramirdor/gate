# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who want AI agents to act on their behalf without handing over an open mandate. Technically literate (they wire up MCP endpoints and bearer tokens), but the editor itself must be calm enough for a non-engineer to fill in a profile. On the editor screen the job is narrow: write the context an agent may read, and decide — per section — exactly how far that context reaches.

## Product Purpose

Gate is a permission wall between AI agents and the human they act for. The profile editor is where the human authors their context sections and sets each one's visibility (public / link / private). Success on this screen: the user trusts that what they see in the preview is *exactly* what an agent receives, and they can adjust reach without second-guessing it.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exact, trustworthy. A guardrail, not a dashboard. Security-product restraint: no gradients, no glow, no decorative motion. Three words: deliberate, legible, unshowy.

## Anti-references

Not a flashy SaaS marketing dashboard (no hero-metric cards, no gradient accents). Not a noisy form builder. Not a "card grid of identical tiles." The accent (amber) is a signal color for state and reach, never decoration.

## Design Principles

- **The preview is the proof.** The "what agents receive" pane is the most important object on the page; the redesign should make the link between editing and reach unmistakable.
- **Reach is first-class.** Visibility is the core decision, not a buried `<select>`. Public/link/private should be glanceable and feel like a deliberate control.
- **Calm over clever.** State (saved, dirty, error) is conveyed quietly. Motion only conveys state.
- **Earned familiarity.** Standard, trustworthy affordances; the tool disappears into the task.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on body text and placeholders. Full keyboard operability for the accordion + reach controls. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Works dark (default) and light.
